# PhD Dissertation, Mohammed Mannan, New York University, August 2026

This repository contains a PDF copy of the PhD dissertation of Mohammed Mannan submitted to New York University. 

**Title:** Preservation of $Q$-energy under quasiconformal homeomorphisms of Ahlfors $Q$-regular limit spaces of admissible inverse systems

**Abstract:** We prove that bi-Lipschitz homeomorphisms between measurable subsets of $X$, an Ahlfors $Q$-regular limit space of an admissible inverse system, preserves $Q$-energy of Lipschitz functions by pullback, from which a standard argument implies that the same result holds for quasiconformal homeomorphisms between open subsets of $X$. Corollaries include preservation of $Q$-energy for bounded Newtonian functions and preservation of $Q$-harmonic functions. For a certain class of $X$ constructed in a self-similar manner, we use the preservation of $Q$-harmonic functions to prove rigidity results for quasiconformal homeomorphisms between open subsets; in particular, such mappings are always local homotheties and can actually be constructed as inverse limits of homomorphisms beween subgraphs of the graph approximations of $X$. We discuss applications of these ideas to the boundaries of certain right-angled Fuchsian buildings.
